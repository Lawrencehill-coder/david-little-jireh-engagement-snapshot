Revenue Reconciliation Required Before Any Commitment Three conflicting revenue figures exist across the broker submission, the LendingWise platform, and Pedison's independent bank statement analysis. The gap between the broker figure ($6.28M) and Pedison's verified figure ($3.688M) represents a 37–41% inflation. The LendingWise figure of $14.1M is unexplained and conflicts with both. CPA Jerry McNair (Reference J2663 OOFF) is the required source for tax return verification. No commitment, no term sheet, and no committee submission will be made until all three figures are reconciled against the actual tax returns.
Single-Payor Concentration Risk Evans Delivery C accounts for approximately 99% of JDS Express revenue as verified through the 13-month Ameris Bank analysis. This represents extreme customer concentration and is a standalone disqualifying condition under standard SBA 7(a) underwriting unless mitigated. Loss of the Evans Delivery contract would effectively eliminate the business's revenue base.
